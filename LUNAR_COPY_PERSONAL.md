## Phase 1: New Moon (0% to 3% Illumination)
*Rhythm: New Moon*

### Strategic Theme & Frequency
- **Theme Title**: Planting The Seeds Of Who You're Becoming
- **Frequency Subtitle**: The Emptiness To Receive & The Delicious Gumption To Ask
- **Energy Focus Pill**: Incubation, spacious reflection, quiet knowing

### Meaning For Your Life (Narrative)
This is where the soul schools the human, darling. The sky is dark, nothing is showing, and that's the whole point. Put the to-do list to one side for a moment and dare to imagine a life so limitless and stretchy it gives you butterflies. We aren't doing vague lottery-ticket daydreaming here. We're planting roots deep into the actual earth, into the floor under your kitchen table and the soles of your feet, so what you long for has living ground to stand on.

### Question For You My Love... (Strategic Inquiry)
What do you long for that switches you on, turns you up and makes you a little uncomfortable all at the same time? And how can you anchor it into your real life today, the one with the washing up and the Wednesday plans?

### How to Plan: Focus Areas
- Dream up the feeling of your next cycle: how you want your mornings, your days and your evenings to feel
- Protect your quiet incubation bubble before you tell anyone anything
- Check your head's clarity against your body's full juicy YES

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Telling the whole world before the inner container has actually formed.
- Asking for opinions from people who can barely manage their own Tuesday.
- Scrolling for inspiration when the answer is already humming inside you.

### What to Do: Little Things
- [ ] Write the raw, unfiltered list of what you want your life to feel like. Feelings first, details after.
- [ ] Look at one thing you've been half-heartedly saying yes to, the one you already know is going nowhere, and lovingly let it go.
- [ ] Map the first three small steps that would give your new seed a real form.

### Today's Tiny Mission
Open a blank page and write down the one thing you most want to welcome into your life. Don't edit it. Don't shrink it. Write without filters.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves
- **Somatic Root**: See a light being switched on at the base of your spine and the soles of your feet at the same time. Feel how this activates your connection to the earth beneath you. Pause and allow yourself to feel solid and stable through this connection.
- **Core Transmission**: When we speak the language of new beginnings, we speak the language of the unknown. Dream big enough so you can root deeply into the core of your earth.
- **Wisdom Excerpt**: Notice the continuous communication between your feet and the earth beneath you. Your conscious awareness of the life beneath you invites a reflection from the earth's intelligence. This gives an opening for you, as an individual physical being, to be schooled by the infinite physical being.

## Phase 2: Early Waxing Moon (4% to 49% Illumination)
*Rhythm: Waxing Moon*

### Strategic Theme & Frequency
- **Theme Title**: Connecting the Dots & Making Contact
- **Frequency Subtitle**: Micro-Commitments & First Steps
- **Energy Focus Pill**: Curious, responsive, gently moving outward

### Meaning For Your Life (Narrative)
Just because only a sliver of the moon is showing doesn't mean the rest has gone on holiday. In your life, this phase is all about making contact with the path ahead. Momentum that's aligned doesn't need a big song and dance. Send three warm voice notes to people you value, take a ten minute walk you didn't plan, say the thing out loud that you've only been thinking. That's enough to start a real current in the field and send a ripple outward.

### Question For You My Love... (Strategic Inquiry)
Which single dot can you connect today that tells life you're switched on, magnetic and open to what's coming?

### How to Plan: Focus Areas
- Reach out to the people who feed you, and start simply by saying hi
- Try things out in real life before you decide anything, one small experiment at a time
- Build the simple daily rhythm that keeps you humming without burning you out

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Designing a twelve step morning routine before you've tried one thing for three days in a row.
- Waiting for 100% certainty before you let yourself begin.

### What to Do: Little Things
- [ ] Send 3 voice messages to people you love and have been meaning to reach.
- [ ] Sketch out what a gentle week could look like, with room for you in it.
- [ ] Put one thing in the diary that is just for you, and treat it like an appointment you'd never cancel.

### Today's Tiny Mission
Record a 90 second voice note to someone you adore. No agenda, zero pressure and 100% warmth.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves
- **Somatic Root**: Skeletal alignment: Bring your attention to your spine, pelvis, and rib cage, see them lighting up and feel how they are fully supported by the ground beneath you.
- **Core Transmission**: As soon as you take an action that feels activated, you begin connecting dots in the field around you. As you do this consistently you increase your access and your capacity.
- **Wisdom Excerpt**: When the moon is waxing, we can only see a sliver of silver; the rest is slowly coming out of the dark. In reflection, you're opening up to reveal more and more of your own capacity through the small expressions and moments of presence that, over time, become a tidal wave.

*Rhythm: Waxing Moon*

### Strategic Theme & Frequency
- **Theme Title**: Integrity & Decisive Action
- **Frequency Subtitle**: Strengthening Boundaries, Cutting Fence Sitting & Strongly Saying 'I Will...'
- **Energy Focus Pill**: Steady, focused, self-honouring

### Meaning For Your Life (Narrative)
The halfway mark to full light always brings a little friction test. Doubts love to creep in right here to check whether your promise to yourself has a spine. Stand tall, stop tolerating the fuzzy boundaries, and make the clean call you've been circling for weeks. Decision fatigue happens with a pile of maybes. Clear one and watch how much lighter your whole body feels.

### Question For You My Love... (Strategic Inquiry)
Where is hesitation costing you your peace of mind, and what clean decision would clear the air immediately?

### How to Plan: Focus Areas
- Remove the small bottlenecks that make every morning harder than it needs to be
- Keep the promises you make to yourself, the way you would for someone you love
- Make a swift, decisive call on the wishy-washy maybes that have been hanging around

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Saying yes when your whole body is saying no.
- Putting off the uncomfortable conversation, the form, the appointment or the reply.

### What to Do: Little Things
- [ ] Decide on the maybe that's been hanging in the air, and give a clear answer.
- [ ] Say no to one invitation that would clutter the hours you love most.
- [ ] Fix, or ask for help with, the one niggling thing you've been grumbling about for weeks.

### Today's Tiny Mission
Make the decision you've been circling all week. Send the message, and don't look back.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves
- **Somatic Root**: Become aware of your in breath as you expand your rib cage and lengthen your spine; feel the grounded command of your skeletal frame.
- **Core Transmission**: Take this expanded and lengthened frame and allow yourself to feel empowered by it. Become aware of the integrity that hums and vibrates at the centre of it.
- **Wisdom Excerpt**: Allow everything that is giving you structure, everything that is giving you form, everything holding you together to be filled with your light. Let this light amplify your security and your trust in the life that flows through you and animates you.

- Warm up the people around you and build delicious anticipation for something you're about to celebrate

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Starting a whole new project or rearranging your life mid-stride.

### What to Do: Little Things

- [ ] Plan the little celebration or gathering you'd love to have at the full moon, and send the invitations.
- [ ] Tell the people close to you what you're stepping into, so they can step in with you.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves

- **Wisdom Excerpt**: Witness that on this entire globe, your pathways are activated. Open up to grow and expand your perspective on what you have the capacity to receive.

## Phase 7: Mid Waning Moon (50% Illumination / Half-lit)
*Rhythm: Waning Moon*

### Strategic Theme & Frequency
- **Theme Title**: The Sacred 'No' & Lovely Clearing Out
- **Frequency Subtitle**: Cord Cutting, Gentle Pruning & Setting Your Boundaries
- **Energy Focus Pill**: Discerning, releasing, liberating, holding your ground

### Meaning For Your Life (Narrative)
Time for a gentle but very thorough spring clean. You can't step into your next chapter while dragging expired commitments, draining dynamics and a phone full of things you stopped caring about in 2022. Saying a clear, loving 'no' to what drains you is the only way to say a thunderous 'yes' to your next level of living.

### Question For You My Love... (Strategic Inquiry)
What tolerated drain on your time, energy, wallet or sanity are you lovingly cutting off today?

### How to Plan: Focus Areas
- Cancel the zombie subscriptions and anything quietly draining you
- Clear the clutter: your wardrobe, your camera roll, your inbox, your desktop
- Renegotiate or step back from commitments and dynamics that no longer fit, with clarity and grace

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Holding onto obligations out of people-pleasing or guilt.
- Starting fresh commitments before you've cleared the messy old slate.

### What to Do: Little Things
- [ ] Look through your bank statement and cancel anything recurring you haven't used in 60 days.
- [ ] Politely decline, or step back from, one commitment or dynamic that leaves you drained.
- [ ] Clear and archive your desktop, your downloads folder and your inbox.

### Today's Tiny Mission
Cancel 2 recurring subscriptions you haven't used in two months, and close the browser tabs you were done with ages ago.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves
- **Somatic Root**: Navel point cord clearing: Bring your attention to your navel point (belly button), this is your point of power. Make a point of releasing any active contractions that limit your expression and keep you small.
- **Core Transmission**: Say no to being minimised. When you say no to what no longer serves you, you say a deeper, sovereign yes to life.
- **Wisdom Excerpt**: This is a space where we get to clean and cleanse. Release the spaces where you hold onto past hurts. Allow the letting go process to happen with the utmost grace and ease.

### Strategic Theme & Frequency
- **Theme Title**: Emptying the Vessel & Lush Restfulness
- **Frequency Subtitle**: Deep Biological Replenishment & Sacred Spaciousness
- **Energy Focus Pill**: Restorative, quiet, inward-looking, gentle

### Meaning For Your Life (Narrative)
The dark phase right before the next seed sprouts. Capable women burn out when they treat this phase like a failure instead of the biological necessity it is. Put the phone down, sink into the couch, let the day be a little bit empty, and allow your subconscious to weave the next breakthrough in the silence. Nothing is wrong. You're just between breaths.

### Question For You My Love... (Strategic Inquiry)
How deliciously spacious can you make your day today, so your nervous system is fully primed for what's next?

### How to Plan: Focus Areas
- Deep physical and nervous system restoration without a drop of guilt
- Spacious contemplation without any pressure to produce something from it
- A gentle look at how your life feels overall: your body, your home, your rhythm, your heart

### How to Plan: What to Lovingly Step Away From
- Panicking over quiet days or a slower pace.
- Forcing epiphanies when your body is begging for rest.

### What to Do: Little Things
- [ ] Block out guilt-free buffer time with nothing in it and nobody to answer to.
- [ ] Restore yourself somatically: long walks, bodywork, a bath, or extra sleep.
- [ ] Look back over this cycle and quietly celebrate how far you've come.

### Today's Tiny Mission
Choose tomorrow morning and leave it completely unscheduled, for rest, a long walk or some pure daydreaming.

### Somatic Root & Strategic Moves
- **Somatic Root**: Whole-body relaxation: Releasing muscular holding patterns into the supportive ground.
- **Core Transmission**: Rest is not a reward for work completed; it's the fertile ground from which everything that's next will spring.
- **Wisdom Excerpt**: When the moon is waning down to dark, we are entering the temple of restoration. Emptying the vessel so the next divine frequency can enter without friction.
